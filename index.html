Who We Are

A group built around purpose.

AGYENOVA GROUP is an organization established with a vision to create value through enterprise, innovation and social impact. We believe successful organizations can create economic value while contributing positively to society.


Our work is guided by integrity, professionalism, collaboration and a long-term commitment to sustainable development.

“We seek to build an organization where enterprise meets purpose — creating opportunities, empowering people and contributing to a better future.”
